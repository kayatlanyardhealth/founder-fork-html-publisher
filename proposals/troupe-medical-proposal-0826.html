Included vs. not included

Where the line sits

Included in every application
  • Full lifecycle management — intake, document collection, CAQH review, provider outreach, and submission
  • Payer follow-up every 15 business days, for up to 180 days after submission
  • One correction-and-resubmission cycle for ordinary payer feedback
  • Written status updates and closure documentation for every application
  • A definitive written outcome — approval and effective date, or a clear record of next options
Not included
  • Guarantees of payer approval or processing timelines
  • State professional licensing, DEA registration, or hospital privileging
  • Payer contract rate negotiation or closed-panel appeals
  • Rework from inaccurate practice information, billed at $95/hr after the included cycle
  • Payer, state, and federal filing fees, passed through at cost
